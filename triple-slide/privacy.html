TripleSlide – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

TripleSlide ("the game") is a block-sliding puzzle game made by Hakan Özer (hozer), an independent developer.

What the game stores

The game keeps your progress on your device so you can continue later: unlocked levels, gold, lives, boosters (undo, extra moves, bomb), tutorial progress and the end time of any infinite-lives period. This data stays on your device and is not sent to the developer.

In-app purchases

Optional in-app purchases are handled by the app store you downloaded the game from (Google Play or the Apple App Store). Payment is processed entirely by the store; the game never sees your card or payment details. The store's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts, no login and no online features.
  • It does not collect personal information and does not send your data to the developer.
  • It contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's own handling of technical data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

All game data lives on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
