New Star Keeper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

New Star Keeper ("the game", Android package com.hozer.newstarkeeper) is a goalkeeper game made by Hakan Özer (hozer), an independent developer.

What the game stores

The game keeps your progress on your device so you can continue later: coins, best score, gloves, settings (sound, vibration, language), your career saves (the keeper name and nationality you type in, clubs, matches, attributes and trophies) and the identifiers of your in-app purchases, so a purchase is never granted twice. This data stays on your device and is not sent to the developer.

In-app purchases

Coin packs and the starter pack are bought through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the game never sees your card or payment details. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts, no login and no online features.
  • It does not collect personal information and does not send your data to the developer.
  • It contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's own handling of technical data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

All game data lives on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it. Career saves can also be deleted inside the game.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
