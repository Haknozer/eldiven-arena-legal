Eldiven Arena – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Eldiven Arena ("the game") is a goalkeeper game made by Hakan Özer, an independent developer.

What the game stores

The game keeps your progress so you can continue later: coins, best score, the gloves you own and use, and your settings (sound, vibration, language). This data is stored on your device, or in the storage that the TikTok Mini Games platform provides to the game. It is not sent to the developer.

TikTok account

When you play inside TikTok, the game may use TikTok's login to get an anonymous identifier for your account, only to keep your progress. The game does not receive your name, contacts, messages, photos or location.

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect personal information.
  • It does not sell or share data with third parties.
  • It does not contain third-party tracking.

TikTok's own handling of your data is described in TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
